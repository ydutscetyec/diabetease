# Manuscript issues to fix before submission

Found while building against the manuscript. Each one is checkable in your own file.

## Blocking

1. **"Data Management and Preprocessing" describes the wrong study.**
   The paragraph reads: *"...transform the collected raw **HIV datasets** into a
   structured, high-quality analytical dataset..."* and lists "mode of transmission"
   as a variable. This is pasted from a different paper. Rewrite it for diabetes
   variables, or delete it.

2. **Phase count contradicts itself.**
   "The study will be conducted in six major phases" is immediately followed by
   "The study will be conducted in seven major phases". Delete the first sentence.

3. **Title says "Forecasting"; the method cannot deliver it.**
   Forecasting future onset requires longitudinal data. The Methodology already
   concedes this ("the model will not be assumed to forecast future Type 1
   diabetes development unless suitable longitudinal data... support that purpose").
   Either secure longitudinal data or retitle. A reviewer will find this.

4. **Objective 1 lists islet autoantibodies as a model input.**
   Autoantibody testing is not routine in South Cotabato, so a local dataset will
   almost certainly lack it. Mark it explicitly conditional, or drop it from the
   objective and keep it in the Scope as a desirable variable.

### One thing to know about Tyler et al.

That system was trained on glucose data generated by a simulator and recommends
weekly insulin dose adjustments. Your methodology already says DiabetEASE will
not reproduce its dosing logic, which is the right call. Cite it for the idea of
evaluating recommendations for safety, not as a model for risk assessment.
